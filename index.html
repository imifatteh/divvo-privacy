Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Divvo app (hereby referred to as "Application"), created by Imtiaz Fatteh ("Service Provider") as a free service intended for use "AS IS".

Data Controller

The Service Provider, Imtiaz Fatteh, is the data controller responsible for personal data collected through the Application.

Information We Collect

To create and use an account, the Application collects the following information:

  • Name – displayed to other users within shared bills
  • Email address – used for authentication and account management
  • Phone number – required to create and secure your account, and to enable participation in shared bills and group-based features
  • User ID – a unique identifier tied to your account
  • Photos – receipt images you upload for bill processing
  • User-generated content – bill data, items, and claims you create within the Application

The Application does not collect precise location data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or analytics data.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to operate the Application:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate users securely
  • Enable bill splitting and group-based functionality
  • Display user identity within shared bills
  • Process receipt images you upload
  • Enable user-initiated connections between users who already know each other

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data monetization.

Contact Information Usage

Phone numbers are used strictly for account creation, authentication, and participation in shared bill features. Users may connect with other users only through user-initiated actions within the Application. The Application does not automatically match or connect users and does not access or upload device contacts.

Tracking

We do not track users across other apps or websites and do not use advertising identifiers.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses Firebase (Google) for authentication, cloud database storage, and file storage. Your data is processed and stored according to Google’s privacy and security practices: Google Firebase Privacy Policy.

External payment apps (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Zelle) are opened only by user action and do not receive any data from the Application.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We only share data in the following limited cases:

  • Firebase (required backend infrastructure provider)
  • Legal compliance if required by law

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data only while your account is active. You may delete your account at any time in the Application settings.

Upon deletion, your personal data is permanently removed from our systems. Deletion is typically completed within 30 days.

Your Rights

You may access or delete your data by managing your account or contacting us at admin@divvoapp.com.

Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it promptly.

International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed on servers outside your country, including the United States, through Firebase infrastructure.

Security

We implement reasonable security measures and rely on Firebase’s industry-standard security infrastructure. However, no system is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page. Continued use of the Application constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-03-14.
