This Privacy Policy applies to the Divvo app (hereby referred to as the "Application"), created by Divvo LLC ("Company," "Service Provider," "we," "us," or "our") as a service intended for use "AS IS".

Data Controller

Divvo LLC is the data controller responsible for the personal information collected through the Application. You can reach us at admin@divvoapp.com.

Information We Collect

To create and use an account, the Application collects the following information:

  • Name – displayed to other users within shared bills.
  • Email address – used for authentication and account management.
  • Phone number – required to create and secure your account and enable participation in shared bills.
  • User ID – a unique identifier associated with your account.
  • Photos – receipt images you voluntarily upload for processing.
  • User-generated content – bill data, claimed items, and other information you create within the Application.

The Application does not collect precise location data, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or analytics data.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to operate and improve the core functionality of the Application.

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate users securely.
  • Enable bill splitting and group-based functionality.
  • Display user identity within shared bills.
  • Process uploaded receipt images.
  • Allow users to connect with other users through user-initiated actions.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data monetization.

Contact Information Usage

Phone numbers are used solely for account creation, authentication, and participation in shared bill features. Users may connect with one another only through user-initiated actions within the Application.

The Application does not access, upload, or synchronize your device contacts.

Tracking

We do not track users across third-party apps or websites and do not use advertising identifiers.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses Firebase (Google) to provide authentication, cloud database storage, and file storage. Your information is processed and stored according to Google's privacy and security practices.

For more information, please visit: Google Firebase Privacy Policy .

AI Processing

To provide receipt scanning and bill-splitting functionality, receipt images that you upload are securely transmitted to Anthropic for processing. Anthropic's AI is used solely to extract receipt text, item names, prices, quantities, and other information necessary to generate and organize your bill.

Receipt images are transmitted to Anthropic only when you choose to upload a receipt for processing. The information is processed only to provide the requested functionality within the Application.

Receipt images and extracted receipt data are not used by the Application for advertising or marketing purposes.

For more information about Anthropic's privacy practices, please visit: Anthropic Privacy Policy .

External payment applications such as Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, and Zelle are opened only when initiated by the user. The Application does not transmit your personal information to these services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Your information is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Firebase (authentication, cloud database, and file storage).
  • Anthropic (AI-powered receipt processing).
  • When required by law or to comply with valid legal requests.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information only while your account remains active.

You may delete your account at any time through the Application settings. Upon account deletion, your personal information is permanently removed from our systems. Deletion is typically completed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete your information by using the account management features within the Application or by contacting us at admin@divvoapp.com.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA) New

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), gives you certain rights over your personal information. Subject to applicable exceptions, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose, and why.
  • Delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so no action is needed to exercise this right.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

In the preceding 12 months, we have collected the categories of personal information described in "Information We Collect" above, for the business purposes described in "How We Use Your Information." We have not sold or shared any personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at admin@divvoapp.com. We will verify your request using the information associated with your account before responding. You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf in accordance with applicable law.

EU/UK Privacy Rights (GDPR) New

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal information under the following legal bases: performance of a contract (to provide the Application's core functionality), your consent (for example, when you choose to upload a receipt), and our legitimate interests (for example, securing the Application and preventing fraud).

Subject to applicable exceptions, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Erase your personal information ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to our processing of your personal information.
  • Port your personal information to another service in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at admin@divvoapp.com.

Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will promptly delete it.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country, including in the United States, through Firebase and Anthropic's infrastructure. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect personal information transferred internationally.

Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information and rely on the industry-standard security infrastructure provided by Firebase and Anthropic.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
